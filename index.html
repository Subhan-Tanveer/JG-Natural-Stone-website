04 The 7–10 Day Process

Four steps. One seamless surface.

  1. Day 1
    01

    Digital Template

    We laser-measure your space to a tolerance most fabricators can't match — every corner, overhang, and sink captured as data.

  2. Days 2–5
    02

    03

    04
